# Aider (Karbon): positioning, AI agents, period close automation

## Raw evidence

### Company context

Aider was founded in New Zealand in 2018 as an AI-powered advisory
platform for accounting and bookkeeping firms. Karbon acquired Aider
on September 30, 2025, to accelerate its AI vision. Karbon is a
practice management platform for accounting firms.

Post-acquisition, Aider's period close automation was rebuilt and
went live for all US customers on April 27, 2026. AU/NZ availability
is planned for later in 2026.

### What Karbon + Aider does today (shipped, live)

**Period Close Automation (powered by Aider)**

- Unified dashboard: single view of every client's close status
  across the firm portfolio, filterable by period type, industry.
- Smart checklists: alert pane surfaces where work needs attention —
  unreconciled transactions, data quality issues, outstanding
  invoices/bills, P&L and balance sheet variances.
- Inline editing: fix a miscategorized transaction, leave a note,
  mark items reviewed — changes push directly to QuickBooks.
  No switching systems.
- Custom rule builder: create checks based on account type, balance
  thresholds, transaction attributes, variance percentages.
- Templates by industry/service/client type. Best-practice template
  out of the box, fully customizable.
- Karbon workflow integration: connect any Aider checklist to a
  Karbon work item. Close progress, outstanding alerts, and %
  completion sync into the integrated task. When a period is marked
  closed in Aider, it updates the work item status and triggers
  Karbon automations.

**Client Questions (August 5, 2026)**

- Raise a question directly from a transaction inside Period Close
  with full context attached.
- Clients respond through the Karbon for Clients portal.
- Every reply returns to the transaction it belongs to.
- Queue questions into a single end-of-day email, send immediately,
  or schedule delivery.
- Track question status from draft through resolved.

**AI Document Validation (August 5, 2026)**

- Compares uploaded documents against what was requested.
- Helps clients catch incorrect uploads before submission.

### What Karbon is marketing / roadmap (announced, not yet shipped)

**Karbon AI Agents** (announced at Karbon Next, June 3, 2026)

- Agents modeled after roles inside an accounting firm:
  - Admin (prepares returns, applies compliance rules)
  - Fractional CFO (builds forecasts, models what-if scenarios,
    client-ready reports)
  - Client Onboarding Specialist (collects info, validates entities,
    configures workflows)
  - Service Delivery Agents (bookkeeping, payroll, tax)
  - Service Management Agents (billing, triage, onboarding)
- AI Agent Management System:
  - Enable/disable specific agents and capabilities
  - Control data access per agent
  - Complete audit trail of every agent decision
  - Approve, edit, or reject agent outputs (trains agents over time)
- "Human-in-control approach" — nothing executes without sign-off.
- 2026 plan: first wave of agents for bookkeeper and fractional CFO
  roles, then email triage, billing, client onboarding.
- "Ask Karbon" / "Kai" — conversational interface that understands
  intent and orchestrates the right agent. Launched at Karbon Next
  2026 in early access.

**Kai** (announced June 3, 2026)

- "AI coworker built directly into the platform where client history,
  workflows, and communications already live."
- CEO quote: "AI multiplies when it's inside the tools you already
  use."
- Features announced:
  - AI notetaker (joins meetings, captures transcripts, surfaces
    structured analysis in Karbon)
  - Public MCP server (connects Karbon to external AI tools via open
    standard)
  - AI analytics (embedded AI in Karbon Analytics)
  - Agentic period close checks (auto-flags transactions needing
    additional action)
- Looking ahead: agentic workflows embedded inside existing firm
  workflows.

### Positioning / language

- "AI-powered teammates that operate inside your existing workflows"
- "Agents modeled after roles that already exist inside an
  accounting firm"
- "Human-in-control" / "nothing executes without your sign-off"
- Karbon AI is "a suite of artificial intelligence features built
  directly into Karbon's practice management platform"
- "The longer your firm runs on Karbon, the smarter Kai gets"
- Account admins can disable AI globally; Agent Management System
  gives per-agent control.

### Pricing / access

- Period Close Automation: available to all US customers (live).
- AI Agents: early beta with select firms. First wave planned 2026.
- Kai: early access announced June 2026.
- Karbon practice management pricing not captured in this pass.

## Notes

### Key observations

1. **Shipped vs marketed**: The live product is period close
   automation — checklists, inline editing, client questions, document
   validation. The "AI Agents" and "Kai" are announced/early beta,
   not generally available. Large gap between marketing and shipping.

2. **Practice management first, AI second**: Karbon's moat is being
   where firms already manage work. AI is an intelligence layer on
   top of existing workflows, not a replacement for them.

3. **Role-based agent framing**: Agents modeled after firm roles
   (bookkeeper, fCFO, onboarding specialist). Researcher later
   corrected the Zapier analogy — see `ev-comp-aider-002`. The
   marketed object is a Viktor-like role/employee, not a workflow
   graph. The shipped reality (Period Close checklist) is still
   closer to workflow automation. Shipped ≠ marketed.

4. **Human-in-control**: Consistent messaging — approve, edit,
   reject agent outputs. Audit trail via Agent Management System.
   Contrasts with Notion's promised-but-missing activity logs.

5. **Xero implications**: Aider's period close works with "the most
   popular general ledgers" — confirmed QuickBooks integration. Xero
   integration not explicitly mentioned in this pass but likely given
   NZ/AU origins. Karbon positions as GL-agnostic practice
   management; XeroForce would be GL-specific.

6. **MCP server**: Karbon announced a public MCP server, allowing
   firms to bring Karbon data into external AI tools. Same pattern
   as Zapier MCP and Digits MCP. AI-as-tool-layer, not just
   AI-as-feature.
