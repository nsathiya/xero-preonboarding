# Karbon: role-based agents, 40-hour claim, workflow-defined accounting

## Raw evidence

Follow-up to `ev-comp-aider-001`. Researcher notes from reading
Karbon's accounting articles, especially
https://karbonhq.com/resources/karbon-ai-agents/

### Role-based agents (not a Zapier-style workflow builder)

Researcher expected Aider/Karbon AI to look more like Zapier
(explicit workflow). The marketed agent model is closer to Viktor:
named roles that already exist in a firm.

From Karbon AI Agents page, agents "are modeled after roles that
already exist inside an accounting firm and equipped with
capabilities mapped to the tasks that role performs." Quoted
examples:

- Bookkeeper: Prepares workpapers, reconciles transactions, and
  follows up with clients for missing documents.
- Tax Admin: Prepares returns, applies compliance rules, and
  drafts client summaries.
- Fractional CFO: Builds forecasts, models ‘what-if’ scenarios,
  and creates client-ready reports.
- Client Onboarding Specialist: Collects information, validates
  entities, and configures workflows automatically.

Same page classifies agents into two groups:

1. Service Delivery Agents that perform the work (bookkeeping,
   payroll, tax).
2. Service Management Agents that run the business behind the
   work (billing, triage, onboarding).

Status on that page: "showcasing AI Agent capabilities in early
beta with select firms." First wave planned 2026: bookkeeper and
fractional CFO, then email triage, billing, client onboarding.

### 40 hours per employee each month

Karbon CEO Mary Delaney, on the Aider acquisition
(https://karbonhq.com/resources/karbon-acquires-aider/):

> Our research shows that firms using AI at an advanced level are
> saving up to 40 hours per employee each month.

Same page, About Karbon: "customer-reported savings of 19.9 hours
per week per employee" (Karbon practice management, not the AI
Agents product). Do not mix the two numbers.

The 40-hour figure is Karbon's own research about advanced AI
users in general, not an independently verified Aider or Kai
outcome. Going Concern (2025 State of AI in Accounting Report
coverage) reported an average closer to 18 hours/month, with
advanced users saving more.

### What else Aider is claimed to cover (beyond period close)

Acquisition page lists, with Aider integrated into Karbon:

- Automated period close
- AI-generated management reports (real-time insights, KPIs,
  executive commentary)
- AI-powered advisory assistant (analytics, client meeting
  prep, internal upskilling)
- Firm-wide dashboards for advisory opportunities
- AI agents that execute tasks (coming soon)

Researcher question, not a product claim: XeroForce is focusing
on agentic workflows. What about client reports, operations
across the full client lifecycle (onboarding, tax, advisory,
billing)?

### Accounting is workflow-defined (researcher observation)

Researcher: Karbon's accounting articles are a good source.
Tutorial videos talk about both the **workflow** and the **tool**.

Accounting work is very workflow-defined. That is in contrast
with MSPs, where training often sells how great the tools are
while workflows vary a lot — a large industry problem. The hope
on the MSP side (researcher paraphrase) is that compliance will
reduce that variance.

This is a researcher observation from Karbon content, not a
quoted Karbon claim.

## Notes

The interesting correction: the *shipped* Aider product is still
period-close checklists (`ev-comp-aider-001`). The *marketed*
agent product is role-based teammates. Expecting Zapier DNA
because Karbon is a workflow/practice system is reasonable; the
article sells Viktor DNA (Bookkeeper, Tax Admin, fCFO,
Onboarding Specialist).

The role list is also a scope map. It is not only "reconcile
transactions." It is workpapers, client follow-up, returns,
forecasts, client-ready reports, entity validation, workflow
setup. That is the full client lifecycle, not a single agentic
workflow.

Do not treat "40 hours per employee each month" as a measured
XeroForce target without checking the underlying research. Use
it as a competitor-stated benchmark and keep the 19.9 hrs/week
Karbon-PM number separate.
